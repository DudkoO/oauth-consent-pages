Dudko Teaching Assistant — Google Integration

This is a personal, single-user application used by an informatics teacher to prepare lesson materials. It connects to Google services through the Google APIs on behalf of the app owner only.

What the app does

  • Creates and edits the owner's Google Slides presentations for lessons.
  • Creates Google Forms quizzes and reads their responses.
  • Organizes the owner's lesson files in Google Drive (folders per class/lesson).

The app runs locally on the owner's own computer and acts strictly on the owner's own Google account. It is not offered to any other users and has no public sign-up.
