Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Dudko Teaching Assistant application ("the app") handles data. The app is a personal, single-user tool operated by Oleg Dudko for his own use only.

Who uses the app

The app is used exclusively by its owner. There is no public registration and no other end users. All access is authorized by the owner's own Google account.

What data the app accesses

With the owner's explicit consent, the app accesses the owner's own Google Slides presentations, Google Forms and their responses, and Google Drive files, solely to create and organize the owner's teaching materials.

How data is used

  • Data is used only to perform actions the owner requests (create slides, create forms, read form responses, organize Drive files).
  • The app does not collect, sell, rent, or share any data with third parties.
  • The app does not use Google user data for advertising or profiling.
  • The app processes data transiently and stores no personal data on any external server; it runs on the owner's local machine.

Data sharing

No Google user data obtained through the app is transferred to any other party, except as required to provide the requested functionality directly with Google's APIs.

Data retention and deletion

The app keeps no long-term database of Google user data. The owner may revoke the app's access at any time via Google Account permissions.
